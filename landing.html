Why It Matters

The Economic Heartbeat of Ghana

Market women form the economic heartbeat of Ghana's informal sector — from food distribution to textiles, retail, and micro-enterprise. Their contribution to household stability and national GDP is enormous, yet the demanding nature of their work means many neglect their own health and well-being.

Odwadini Mpuntuo combines financial wellness, health awareness, appreciation, celebration, and community engagement in one impactful Workers' Day experience.
