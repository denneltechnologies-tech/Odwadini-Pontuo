Our Objectives

What the Day Sets Out to Do

  • Provide on-site health screenings for a large number of market women, enabling early detection of conditions like hypertension and diabetes.
  • Connect health organisations directly with new patients and create clear referral pathways for follow-up care.
  • Build trust with market women through face-to-face interaction, positioning providers as their go-to healthcare partners.
  • Educate traders on simple, practical health habits that fit their daily routines and reduce long-term health risks.
  • Expand community outreach by engaging an underserved, high-impact population within the informal sector.

The Programme & Event Objectives

A full-day wellness-centred celebration designed specifically for traders in all 67 markets in the Greater Accra region — one of the busiest trading hubs in the country.

Event Highlights

Hour by Hour at Makola Market

Six connected experiences flow through the day — wellness at dawn, health and finance by midday, celebration by dusk.

6:00 AM – 9:00 AM

Sunrise Mass Aerobics Session

A dawn fitness session to energise the body and mind before trading stalls open. Warm-ups, guided cardio, and community movement with certified instructors.

9:00 AM – 3:00 PM

Free Health Screenings & Doctor Consultations

On-site medical consultations, blood pressure monitoring, blood sugar & diabetes testing, eye checks, and direct referral pathways to partner hospitals.

9:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Susu Savings & Financial Wellness Masterclass

Actionable financial education tailored for market traders: informal savings discipline, digital mobile money management, micro-insurance, and wealth preservation.

10:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Traditional Fun Games & Community Contests

Oware competitions, ludo, dancing contests, tug-of-war, and laughter — designed for traders to unwind, make friends across markets, and celebrate.

2:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Live Cultural Entertainment & Highlife Music

Vibrant cultural drumming, highlife tunes, guest artist performances, and joyful dancing carrying Makola Market into the evening.

4:30 PM – 6:00 PM

Appreciation & Market Queens Honours Ceremony

Prestigious citation presentations, hampers, and national recognition for veteran market queens and long-serving traders across Greater Accra.

Need Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating, attending, or partnering with Odwadini Mpuntuo.

Is entry and participation really 100% free? +
Yes! Every market woman and trader attends completely free. Screenings (blood pressure, diabetes), mass aerobics, susu financial literacy talks, games, and the appreciation ceremony are all free courtesy of 360 Group Ltd and our event partners.
What health screenings will be conducted on site? +
Licensed healthcare professionals will conduct free blood pressure monitoring, blood glucose/sugar tests, basic vitals, and physician consultations, complete with referral pathways to partner hospitals and clinics.
Can an entire market association register together? +
Yes! Visit our Membership page and select "Association" to register your market queen leadership and member delegation so your group is formally welcomed and allocated designated seating.
How can corporate brands or institutions partner? +
Corporate brands can sponsor packages from Title Partner down to Community Supporter, secure activation booth space, provide screening supplies, or speak on stage. Submit a partnership inquiry or call Daniel Obeng-Fianko directly at 0244 651561.
